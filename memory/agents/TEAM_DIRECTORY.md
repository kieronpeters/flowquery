## Permanent identity rules

- Keep each name, role and portrait filename stable across projects.
- Portraits are visual identity assets only; they do not imply real-world identity or credentials.
- Use `team/portraits/<name>.png` for each profile.
- The contractor is a single anonymous, gender-neutral generic profile at `team/portraits/contractor.png`.
- The contractor has no permanent personal name and is assigned a temporary specialist brief per engagement.
- Application-level user accounts for these AI identities are a future product decision, not part of the current MVP.
